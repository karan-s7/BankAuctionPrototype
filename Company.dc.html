About us

India auctions billions in seized property. Almost nobody can see it clearly.

Every year, lenders across India put tens of thousands of crores of repossessed property up for auction. The notices are public. The information that would let an ordinary buyer act on them is not. BankAuctions.ai exists to close that gap.

What we believe

A distressed asset sale should not be a game of who knows the branch manager. Every buyer deserves the same facts a professional investor would have: what comparable properties actually sold for, whether the keys exist, whether a lender will finance it, and what the street outside is really like.

What we do

We track every auction notice from banks, NBFCs, housing finance companies, co-operative societies, small finance banks, asset reconstruction companies and court-ordered sales across five markets — then verify, deduplicate and enrich each one until it can be trusted.

Who we serve

Buyers who want the whole market in one place with the risk taken out. And lenders who need those buyers to show up, because every failed auction is another quarter of a non-performing asset on the books.

The five mistakes first-time buyers make

Bidding on symbolic possession

If the lender has not physically taken the property, getting the occupant out is your problem and it can take years.

Treating the reserve as the value

The reserve is what the lender needs to recover, not what the asset is worth. Sometimes it is far below market. Sometimes above.

Forgetting the all-in cost

Stamp duty, registration, arrears and refurbishment routinely add 15–20% to the hammer price.

Missing the EMD deadline

The earnest money deposit closes days before the auction. Miss it and you cannot bid, however ready you are.

Assuming finance is automatic

Not every lender funds auction purchases, and not every property qualifies. Check before you commit the EMD.

What is hard to copy

The outcome dataset

Reserve prices are public; clearing prices are not. Every closed lot we capture makes the next valuation better, and the set cannot be bought.

1,100 notice sources

Bank portals, auctioneer platforms, DRT boards and regional newspapers, ingested daily. The pipeline took two years to stabilise.

Two-person verification

No figure publishes until a second person independently confirms it against the source document. It is a cost, and it is the reason buyers pay.

People on the ground

Field engineers and empanelled advocates in each city. Software alone cannot tell you a family still lives in the flat.

What could go wrong

We would rather you read this from us than find it in diligence.

Supply is tied to the credit cycle

Auction volume follows bad loans. A benign cycle shrinks the market. SARFAESI referrals have held near 2.16 lakh for two years, and our services revenue scales per transaction rather than per notice.

Notice access could be restricted

Publication is a statutory requirement under SARFAESI, so notices stay public, but a portal could throttle machine access. We ingest from 1,100 sources including print, and no single one exceeds 9% of volume.

A wrong report damages trust

We refund in full on any material error a buyer can demonstrate, carry professional indemnity cover, and hold every published figure to a second independent check. Four refunds issued to date.

Field quality is hard to scale

Report quality lives with the engineer who visits. Each new city opens with a trained lead before it takes paid work, and reports are sampled weekly.

Transaction revenue is lumpy

Two thirds of the Year-5 model is transaction-linked and a single concierge file can move a month. Subscription revenue is what we manage against.

A portal could build this

The data is copyable in a quarter; the outcome history, field network and advocate panel are not. Our answer is to keep compounding the part that has to be earned.

Governance and compliance

Structure

Private limited company registered in Chennai, GST registered, statutory audit from year one.

We hold no client money

EMD and bid amounts move only between the buyer and the lender's portal, which keeps us outside payment intermediary regulation.

Data protection

Consent-based collection under the DPDP Act, data resident in India, borrower identities never republished beyond the notice.

Independent legal opinions

Written by empanelled advocates in their own name. We take no referral fee that depends on the opinion's conclusion.

Investor reporting

Monthly metrics pack, quarterly management accounts, annual audited statements. One board observer seat in this round.

Loans through partners

We do not lend. Auction finance is arranged through partner lenders on disclosed commission.
