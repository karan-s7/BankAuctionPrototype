How a listing earns the word verified

Verified means somebody actually went there.

Every property on this portal passes through five hands before you see it, and two of them are human eyes that have to agree.

01
Notice captured

The printed sale notice is scanned or taken from the publisher’s e-paper the morning it appears.

02
Machine-read, human-corrected

Lender, reserve, EMD, dates and schedule of property are extracted and corrected by a maker.

03
Checked by a second person

A checker compares every field against the clipping. Nothing publishes on one person’s word.

04
Assigned for a site visit

On approval the property is routed automatically to a field technician in that city, with the address from the notice.

05
Visited, then verified again

The technician uploads dated photographs, a walkthrough video and the GPS pin. The checker verifies those too, and the listing is marked Verified.

Verified photos, the walkthrough video and the GPS pin open with Concierge Services.

The visit costs us money to produce and it is the single thing that tells you whether the flat is occupied, whether the building is maintained and whether the address in the notice is even real. Free accounts see that a visit happened and when. Pro members see what the technician saw.
