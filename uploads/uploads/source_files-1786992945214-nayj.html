The insight

Every portal in India monetises the notice. None monetises the outcome.

In the UK, Essential Information Group has sold exactly this for decades: every property coming to auction and every one already sold — 850,000 properties, 600,000 with results — as a paid membership.

India lists more than twice the UK's annual volume and has no equivalent. That dataset does not exist here yet. Whoever builds it first owns the category.

What could go wrong

Four risks we would rather you heard from us.

Outcome data may be hard to get

The whole moat assumes we can observe hammer prices. If lenders do not disclose and registration lags, the flagship degrades to an estimate. This is precisely what the Chennai pilot tests, before we spend on four more cities.

A free state platform exists

BAANKNET has the public sector monopoly, official trust and no price. We must be complementary — non-PSB coverage, analytics and service — never a paid substitute for something free.

Willingness to pay is unproven

No Indian competitor charges retail buyers for auction data, so our 3% conversion assumption has no local benchmark. The UK proves it on professionals, not on retail.

Concierge dilutes the multiple

Half of modelled revenue is a services business inside a platform valuation. We automate the diligence steps aggressively and present concierge honestly as the channel that buys us proprietary data.
